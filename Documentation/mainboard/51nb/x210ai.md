# 51NB X210AI

Meteor Lake replacement mainboard for the ThinkPad X200/X201 chassis
(Core Ultra 7 165H or 9 185H, DDR5 SO-DIMMs, two M.2 NVMe and a 2.5" SATA bay,
two USB-C with one Thunderbolt 4).

## Flashing

Internal flashing works from Linux on the running board and is by far the
easiest route:

```sh
flashrom -p internal --ifd -i bios -N -w build/coreboot.rom
```

coreboot builds only the BIOS region, so `-i bios` writes just that and leaves
the factory descriptor, ME and Device Expansion 2 regions untouched. Do not
write the whole image. it contains no descriptor or ME.

Flashing externally with an SPI programmer is also possible. I flashed using a CH431B with a 1.8v adapter though this is not recommended. The SPI chip is a WSON-8 on the mainboard, circled here:

![](x210ai.png)

Back up the factory flash before writing anything.

## Untested

- TPM (Intel PTT is not enabled in this ME image)
- Thunderbolt/USB4 tunnelling and WWAN
